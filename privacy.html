Privacy Policy

Welcome to Excipure Pharma ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information obtained through our website and business interactions.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us, requesting quotations, placing orders, or communicating with us.

Personal and Business Information

The information we may collect includes:

  • • Full Name
  • • Company Name
  • • Email Address
  • • Mobile Number
  • • Shipping Address
  • • Billing Address
  • • GST Details (where applicable)
  • • Product Requirements
  • • Order and Delivery Information

Technical Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • • IP Address

  • • Device Information

This information is collected through Google Analytics and similar technologies.

2. Purpose of Information Collection

We collect and process information for the following purposes:

  • • Responding to inquiries and quotation requests
  • • Processing product orders
  • • Managing shipping and delivery requirements
  • • Providing customer support
  • • Maintaining business records
  • • Improving website performance and user experience
  • • Monitoring website traffic through Google Analytics
  • • Complying with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations

We only collect information necessary for legitimate business purposes.

3. How We Store Information

Customer and inquiry information may be stored through:

  • • Secure Email Systems
  • • Google Sheets
  • • Internal Business Records

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

Excipure Pharma does not sell, rent, or trade customer information to third parties.

Information may be shared only when necessary with:

  • • Shipping and logistics partners

Such sharing is limited to information necessary for the intended purpose.

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior and improve website performance. Google Analytics may collect information such as:

6. Data Retention

We retain customer and inquiry information only for as long as necessary to:

  • • Fulfill orders
  • • Maintain business records
  • • Meet tax and regulatory obligations

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to safeguard personal information, including:

  • • Restricted access to customer information

While we strive to protect your information, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Subject to applicable Indian laws, you may request:

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

Our website and services are intended for business and commercial purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

10. Compliance with Indian Law

Excipure Pharma endeavors to comply with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and other relevant regulations governing the collection, storage, and processing of personal data.
